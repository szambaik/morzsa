# Következő ikonok

Mindegyik prompt mellé az eredeti Morzsa-ikonsorozatot csatold referenciának. Két 12-es csomag, majd egy külön ikon lefedi a 27 kiemelt hiányzó terméket; a sonka és a darált hús családikon több termékhez használható.

## Hús, pékáru, tejtermék

Create 12 NEW icons extending the attached Morzsa collection. Match its matte handcrafted clay, warm cream and forest-green packaging, soft upper-left light and front three-quarter view. Bold silhouettes and minimal texture, readable at 36–40 px. Exact order, left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: raw chicken breast fillet; raw minced meat in a simple tray; overlapping slices of cooked ham.
Row 2: thick slices of bacon; two plain frankfurter sausages; curved smoked sausage.
Row 3: cottage cheese with visible white curds in an open tub; small cream carton; spreadable cream cheese in a shallow open tub with a smooth swirl.
Row 4: mayonnaise jar with pale contents; round bread roll; Hungarian crescent bread roll, smooth bread crust, NOT a flaky croissant.
Exactly 3 columns × 4 rows, portrait 3:4 canvas, equal square cells. Centre each object at 65% cell coverage, with generous empty margins. Transparent background. No grid, tiles, labels, text, logos, faces, extra props, overlapping cells or cropped edges. Preserve the reference style, not stock emoji style.
