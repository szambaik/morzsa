# Morzsa visual identity

Warm pantry-paper surfaces, a prominent irregular orange crumb/sun mark, and
compact, quick-to-use product cards. Keep the brand friendly and tactile.

- Mark: assets/brands/morzsa.svg. Use this same silhouette for the header,
  favicon, and quiet decorative accents. Do not replace it with an emoji.
- Paper: #fbf6ee; cards: #fffdf8; ink: #2c3a2e.
- Terracotta action color: #b94b27; pale orange: #f8e4d5.
- Green remains the semantic indication of products present at home.
- Key action shapes use opposing soft and tighter corners, like a small label.
- Prefer one recognizable mark and a few orange accents over more UI decoration.
- Preserve compact card geometry, legible text, touch targets, and reduced motion.
- Keep Google's official sign-in artwork and button treatment independent.
