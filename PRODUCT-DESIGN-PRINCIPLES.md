# Morzsa — Product Design Principles

## Product definition

Morzsa is a lightweight, AI-powered shared household food assistant.

Its purpose is to help household members coordinate groceries, maintain an approximate understanding of available ingredients, and discover meals they can prepare with what they already have.

It is NOT a traditional inventory management application.

## Core philosophy

**Convenience over precision.**

The application should minimize the effort required to maintain useful household information.

Never force users to provide exact quantities, weights, expiration dates or other details unless necessary. Approximate information is valuable and should be treated as a first-class state.

## Core experiences

1. Shared home inventory: a simple representation of what is available at home.
2. Collaborative shopping list: real-time shared editing and synchronization between household members.
3. AI recipe discovery: personalized recipe suggestions based on available ingredients, prioritizing what users already have.
4. Conversational management: users can manage inventory and shopping lists through natural language and voice.

## UX principles

- Minimize interactions and required input.
- Make common actions achievable in one or two taps.
- Support approximate quantities and availability states.
- Make shared changes immediately visible.
- Make the relationship between inventory, shopping list and recipes obvious.
- Avoid unnecessary configuration and administrative workflows.
- Treat AI as an integrated assistant, not a separate destination.
- Always provide understandable feedback when AI modifies household data.

## Visual direction

Organic Kitchen + Playful Companion.

Use the established Morzsa identity: warm cream, sage green, forest green and terracotta; handcrafted food illustrations; friendly typography; rounded components; restrained shadows and subtle animations.

The mascot should represent the AI assistant and provide continuity across the experience.

## Design success criteria

A user should be able to:

- Quickly understand what is at home.
- Add something to the shared shopping list without friction.
- See what another household member has changed.
- Discover what they can cook without manually checking every ingredient.
- Tell Morzsa what they bought or used and have the application update accordingly.

The ultimate goal is to make maintaining the application feel easier than sending a shopping list over a messaging app.
